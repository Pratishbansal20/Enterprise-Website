**Short answer: pick suede or matt for kitchens and children’s rooms because they hide fingerprints and fine scratches, high gloss or acrylic for a rich, shiny look where you don’t mind wiping often, and textured wood-grain where you want the feel of real wood.** Use 1 mm laminate on the faces people see and a thinner liner inside.

## What do the different laminate finishes look like?

| Finish | Look | Shows fingerprints? | Shows scratches? | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Suede / matt** | Soft, low-sheen | Rarely | Hides them well | Kitchens, wardrobes, kids’ rooms |
| **High gloss** | Shiny, reflective | Yes | Fine scratches visible | Living room units, feature shutters |
| **Super high gloss / acrylic** | Mirror-like depth | Yes | Visible; needs care | Premium kitchen shutters, TV units |
| **Textured (wood grain, stone)** | Feels like real wood or stone | Rarely | Hides them well | Wardrobes, wall panelling, beds |
| **Metallic** | Brushed or burnt-metal effect | Some | Some | Accent panels, shop interiors |

Gloss finishes also bounce more light around a room, which helps small or dark spaces, but they show dust sooner.

## Which thickness should I use where?

- **1 mm:** the standard for shutters, table tops and visible sides.
- **0.7–0.8 mm liner:** inside cabinets and wardrobes, where nobody sees it but the board still needs protecting and balancing.
- **1.25–1.5 mm:** heavy-use surfaces or when you want a thicker, more solid feel.

Always put a liner on the back of a laminated board. A board laminated on one side only can slowly bend.

## How do I choose the colour?

1. **See it in person.** Shades look different on a phone screen. Visit our showroom near Bajrangi Chowk and hold samples against each other.
2. **Think about light.** Light shades make small rooms feel bigger; dark gloss looks dramatic but shows dust and smudges.
3. **Match the hardware.** Black handles suit light matt shades; rose-gold and brass suit wood grains and deep colours. See our [furniture fittings](/products/furniture-fittings-hazaribagh/).
4. **Keep it simple.** Two or three laminates per room usually look better than five.

## Does the shape of the furniture matter?

Yes. For curved or rounded edges, ask for a post-forming grade laminate that can bend around the edge without cracking. For straight edges, any decorative laminate works with matching edge banding.

## How do I look after laminate?

- Wipe with a soft, damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid scrubbers and harsh cleaners, especially on gloss and acrylic.
- Peel off the protective film on acrylic and high-gloss sheets only after all fitting work is finished.
- Don’t put very hot pans directly on laminate tops.

## Laminate or veneer?

Laminate is tougher and easier to maintain. Veneer is a thin slice of real wood: each sheet is unique, and it needs polishing after fixing. Many homes use veneer for one feature wall or the main door and laminate everywhere else.
