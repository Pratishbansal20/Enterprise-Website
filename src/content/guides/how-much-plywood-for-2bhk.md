**Short answer: for a typical 2BHK with two wardrobes, two storage beds, an L-shaped modular kitchen and a TV unit, plan for roughly 30 sheets of 18/19 mm plywood, about 8 sheets of 6 mm and 2 sheets of 12 mm.** About 10 of the 18/19 mm sheets should be BWP for the kitchen. Your exact number depends on your design, so treat this as a budgeting guide and get your carpenter’s cutting list before buying.

## How is plywood counted?

Plywood is sold by the sheet. A standard **8×4 ft sheet covers 32 sq ft**. To estimate, add up the area of every panel in the furniture, divide by 32, and add **about 15% for cutting waste**.

## The worked example

These are the assumptions behind the estimate. Your carpenter’s design will differ.

| Item | Size assumed |
| --- | --- |
| Wardrobe (×2) | 6 ft wide × 7 ft high × 2 ft deep, hinged shutters, one partition, six shelves |
| Storage bed (×2) | Queen, about 5 × 6.5 ft, box storage, headboard |
| Kitchen | L-shape: 16 running ft of base units, 10 running ft of wall units |
| TV unit | 6 ft wide, low cabinet plus wall panel |

### One wardrobe

- Sides: 2 × (7 × 2) = 28 sq ft
- Top and bottom: 2 × (6 × 2) = 24 sq ft
- Partition: 7 × 2 = 14 sq ft
- Shelves: 6 × (3 × 2) = 36 sq ft
- Shutters: 6 × 7 = 42 sq ft

**Total 144 sq ft ÷ 32 = 4.5 sheets, + 15% waste ≈ 5–6 sheets of 18/19 mm**, plus **2 sheets of 6 mm** for the back (6 × 7 = 42 sq ft).

### One storage bed

- Platform: 5 × 6.5 ≈ 33 sq ft
- Box sides: 23 ft around × 1.25 ft high ≈ 29 sq ft
- Headboard: 5 × 3 = 15 sq ft

**Total ≈ 77 sq ft ≈ 3 sheets of 18/19 mm**, plus **1 sheet of 12 mm** for the box bottom.

### Kitchen

- Base units work out to about 11 sq ft of board per running foot (sides, bottom, shelf and shutters): 16 × 11 = 176 sq ft ≈ **6–7 sheets**.
- Wall units are shallower, about 8 sq ft per running foot: 10 × 8 = 80 sq ft ≈ **3 sheets**.
- Backs: about **3 sheets of 6 mm**.

**Kitchen total ≈ 10 sheets of 18/19 mm BWP + 3 sheets of 6 mm.** Use BWP here; see [BWP vs MR vs commercial plywood](/guides/bwp-vs-mr-vs-commercial-plywood/).

### TV unit

About **3 sheets of 18/19 mm** and **1 sheet of 6 mm**, depending on how much wall panelling you add.

## Adding it up

| Furniture | 18/19 mm | 12 mm | 6 mm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 wardrobes | 12 | – | 4 |
| 2 storage beds | 6 | 2 | – |
| Kitchen (BWP) | 10 | – | 3 |
| TV unit | 3 | – | 1 |
| **Total (approx.)** | **31** | **2** | **8** |

## What changes the number?

- **Lofts above wardrobes** add 2–3 sheets each.
- **Sliding wardrobes** use slightly less board for shutters but need extra for the track frame.
- **Drawers** add sides, fronts and bottoms: roughly half a sheet for every three or four drawers.
- **Crockery units, shoe racks and study tables** add 2–4 sheets each.
- **Blockboard** is often used instead of plywood for long shelves, bed panels and doors.
